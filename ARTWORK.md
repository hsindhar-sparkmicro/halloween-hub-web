# Asset credits

- `web/assets/art-sheet.png`, `scene-backgrounds.png`, and `monsters.png`: original artwork generated for Halloween Hub using imagegen in October 2026. The atlases contain original Halloween illustrations and environments; characters, fog, and scene events animate locally in canvas/SVG.
- `web/assets/*.wav`: original deterministic audio synthesis created for this project. Personal browser imports are not distributed.
- `web/audio/`: the six music tracks and ten sound effects originally supplied for the kiosk, incorporated into this Halloween experience at the project owner’s request. [Audio manifest](web/audio-manifest.js) records the original filenames, creator handles and Pixabay asset IDs from those filenames, durations, sizes, and SHA-256 hashes. The source pages were not independently retrieved. These files retain the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/); they are integrated soundtrack assets, with no new blanket license or standalone download-library offering.
- DejaVu fonts are bundled with their original [license](web/assets/FONT-LICENSE.txt).
- `web/media/`: the user-provided Halloween welcome, Dracula, Nun, and pumpkin-scare clips and matching background images, included at the project owner's direction. Original MP4/WebM formats are preserved. Their inclusion does not imply a separate open-content license.
- Leaves and snow use original procedural canvas sprites and local animation. Favicon, pumpkin faces, and interface decoration use project SVG/CSS.
- Weather data: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The public free API is used for this non-commercial site.

No product showcase, corporate logo, or product photography is included. Publishing the source does not add a new blanket license to third-party assets.
